Fix MacBook Not Charging

A MacBook that won't charge is frustrating but usually fixable without a trip to the Apple Store. Work through these in order before assuming the hardware is dead.

Start With the Obvious

  • Try a different wall outlet — the outlet itself might be dead
  • Remove any USB hub or dock and plug directly into the MacBook
  • Try a different cable if you have one — USB-C cables fail more than people realise
  • Plug the charger into the other side of the MacBook if it has ports on both sides
  • Check for debris in the USB-C port — compressed air can clear it

Check the Battery Status

Hold Option → click the battery icon in the menu bar

The status shows: Normal, Service Recommended, Service Battery, or Charging. If it says "Service Battery" the battery needs replacing — but the guide below can still help you charge in the meantime.

Check MagSafe LED (Older MacBooks)

  • Green = charged or nearly charged
  • Orange/Amber = currently charging
  • No light = not connected properly, dead cable, or dead adapter

If the LED is green but battery percentage is low and not rising, the SMC needs resetting.

Reset the SMC

The SMC controls power management including charging. Resetting it fixes most charging issues.

Apple Silicon MacBooks: Shut down completely and wait 30 seconds. Power on normally — the SMC resets automatically on shutdown.

Intel MacBook (non-removable battery): Shut down → hold Shift+Control+Option+Power for 10 seconds → release all → power on.

Intel MacBook Pro (T2 chip, 2018 and later): Shut down → hold Right Shift+Left Control+Left Option for 7 seconds, then keep holding those and also press Power for another 7 seconds → release all → wait a few seconds → power on.

Let the Battery Drain Completely

Occasionally the battery controller gets confused about the charge level. Run the battery all the way to 0% until the Mac shuts itself off, then plug in and charge to 100% without interruption.

Check Battery Cycle Count

Apple menu → About This Mac → System Report → Hardware → Power

Look at "Cycle Count" and "Condition". MacBook batteries last around 500–1000 cycles before degrading significantly. After that, charging slows and capacity drops — a replacement battery is the only fix.

Test With a Different Charger

Apple USB-C chargers and MagSafe adapters fail. If a friend's charger works, yours is dead. A 61W or 96W USB-C charger from any reputable brand (Anker, Belkin, Apple) will work on modern MacBooks.

Check System Report for Battery Errors

Apple menu → About This Mac → System Report → Hardware → Power

Look for any error flags under "Battery Information". Also check:

log show --predicate 'subsystem == "com.apple.battery"' --last 1h | grep -i error

When It's Likely Hardware

  • Swollen battery — the MacBook lid lifts slightly at the bottom, or there's a bump under the trackpad
  • Bent or burnt USB-C port — visible damage
  • Charging board failure — multiple cables and chargers tried, nothing works

Book an Apple Store Genius Bar appointment. Swollen batteries are replaced free or at low cost even out of warranty in many regions due to safety regulations.
