Fix Bluetooth Keeps Disconnecting on Mac

Bluetooth disconnections on Mac are almost always caused by interference, corrupted preference files, or a firmware hiccup. Most cases are fixed without reinstalling anything.

Quick Fixes to Try First

  • Turn Bluetooth off and on — click the Bluetooth icon in the menu bar
  • Remove the device and pair it again from scratch
  • Move the device closer — Bluetooth range is roughly 10 metres but drops through walls
  • Disconnect any USB 3.0 devices — they emit interference in the 2.4GHz band that disrupts Bluetooth
  • Switch your Wi-Fi to 5GHz — 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth share the same frequency

Reset the Bluetooth Module

This resets the Bluetooth hardware without losing your paired devices on modern macOS:

# Hold Option + Shift and click the Bluetooth icon in the menu bar
# Click "Reset the Bluetooth module"
# Reconnect your devices

On older macOS versions the option may appear differently or not at all — proceed to the preference file fix instead.

Delete Bluetooth Preference Files

Corrupted Bluetooth preferences cause persistent disconnection issues:

# Quit all apps first, then run:
sudo rm -f /Library/Preferences/com.apple.Bluetooth.plist
sudo rm -f ~/Library/Preferences/com.apple.Bluetooth.plist
sudo rm -f /Library/Preferences/com.apple.bluetoothd.plist

Restart your Mac. macOS recreates these files and you'll need to re-pair your devices.

Reset NVRAM

NVRAM stores Bluetooth state information. Resetting it can fix persistent issues:

Intel Mac: Shut down → power on and immediately hold Option+Command+P+R for about 20 seconds until you hear the startup chime twice.

Apple Silicon: NVRAM resets automatically on boot — just restart.

Check for Interference

  • USB 3.0 hubs and cables are major Bluetooth offenders — try unplugging them
  • Microwave ovens cause temporary interference — keep your Mac away from one when it's running
  • Dense Wi-Fi environments (offices, apartments) cause congestion — nothing to do except use 5GHz Wi-Fi
  • Fluorescent lights and LED strips with poor EMI shielding can also interfere

Update macOS and Device Firmware

Bluetooth issues are often fixed in point releases. System Settings → General → Software Update. For Apple peripherals like AirPods, firmware updates install automatically when connected.

Check the Bluetooth Menu Bar for Clues

# Hold Option and click the Bluetooth icon
# Shows signal strength and detailed status for each device
# Look for "RSSI" — values below -70 indicate a weak signal

For Keyboards and Mice Specifically

If your keyboard or mouse disconnects during sleep/wake:

  1. System Settings → Battery → Options
  2. Disable "Enable Power Nap"
  3. System Settings → Bluetooth → click your device → uncheck "Allow Bluetooth devices to wake this computer" if it appears

Last Resort — Bluetooth USB Dongle

If nothing else works, a Bluetooth 5.0 USB dongle (about $10) bypasses the built-in hardware entirely. Plug it in and macOS uses it automatically. Useful when the built-in chip is failing.
