Fix CarPlay Not Working

CarPlay connects over USB or Wi-Fi. Most issues are either a bad cable, a software state that needs resetting, or a car head unit that needs a firmware update.

USB CarPlay — Check the Cable First

Most CarPlay failures are cheap or damaged cables. CarPlay requires a data-capable cable, not just a charging cable. Use the Apple cable that came with your iPhone or a certified MFi cable. Third-party cables from dollar stores often fail silently.

  1. Try a different cable
  2. Try a different USB port in your car (some ports are charge-only)
  3. Clean the Lightning/USB-C port on your iPhone with a dry toothpick — lint buildup is very common

Check iPhone Settings

  1. Settings → General → CarPlay → confirm your car appears
  2. If it does not appear, connect the cable and wait 15 seconds
  3. If it appears but shows "Not Connected", tap it → Forget This Car → reconnect

Check Car Head Unit Settings

Most cars require CarPlay to be enabled in the head unit settings. Look for:

  • Settings → Connections → Apple CarPlay → Enable
  • Some cars require a dealer activation or paid upgrade for CarPlay

Restart Both Devices

  1. Fully power off your iPhone (not just lock it)
  2. Turn off your car completely, wait 30 seconds
  3. Start the car, then connect your iPhone

Reset CarPlay on iPhone

Settings → General → CarPlay → [Your Car] → Forget This Car

Reconnect by plugging in the cable. The car will prompt to allow CarPlay again.

Wireless CarPlay Not Connecting

  1. Wireless CarPlay requires Wi-Fi and Bluetooth to both be ON
  2. Connect via USB once to pair — wireless should work automatically after
  3. Settings → General → CarPlay → confirm the car is listed
  4. On the car head unit, enable wireless CarPlay in Bluetooth or Wi-Fi settings
  5. Make sure your iPhone is not connected to another Bluetooth device that may interfere

CarPlay Disconnects Randomly

This is almost always the cable. A cable that works for charging can fail intermittently for CarPlay data. Replace the cable with a genuine Apple cable or a certified MFi cable.

Also check:

  • USB port voltage — some aftermarket adapters provide unstable power
  • Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode — disable it, as it can restrict CarPlay

Update Car Firmware

Many CarPlay issues are fixed in head unit firmware updates. Check your car manufacturer's website or the head unit settings for software updates. Some manufacturers (BMW, Mercedes, Honda) push updates over the air; others require a dealer visit.

Reset Network Settings (Last Resort)

Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset Network Settings

This clears Wi-Fi passwords, Bluetooth pairings, and VPN settings. Reconnect your car after this.
